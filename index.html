The cues are amplified through a soft compressor for clarity over podcasts. Your iPhone volume buttons set the final output.

iPhone audio

The cues use Web Audio so they can mix over a podcast or music. The iPhone Silent Switch can still silence these cues even while your podcast continues. If you switch apps, TGintervals pauses and reconnects audio when you return and tap Play. For portrait-only use, add TGintervals to your Home Screen. A Safari tab cannot guarantee portrait; iPhone Portrait Orientation Lock can.
